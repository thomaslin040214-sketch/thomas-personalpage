About Me

Currently in my third year at the University of Sydney, pursuing a Bachelor of Arts majoring in Economics. My research and academic focus center on macroeconomic policy formulation, regulatory enforcement, and market design. By bridging quantitative econometric modeling with institutional economic theory, I investigate how policy instruments influence market efficiency, strategic firm behavior, and long-run economic welfare.

Research & Focus Areas

Industrial Organization

Market Structure & Regulation

Analyzing competition policy, natural monopolies, and the welfare effects of regulatory oversight across concentrated industries.

Applied Econometrics

Empirical & Policy Evaluation

Leveraging quantitative methods and economic data to evaluate policy effectiveness, shock transmissions, and macroeconomic stability.

Microeconomic Theory

Game Theory & Mechanism Design

Examining strategic equilibrium, incentive structures, and Pareto efficiency under informational asymmetry and market externalities.
