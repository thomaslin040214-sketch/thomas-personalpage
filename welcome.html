Welcome & Academic Overview

Candidate for BA in Economics • The University of Sydney

Sydney, Australia

Dear Visitor,

Thank you for visiting my personal research and academic portfolio. I created this space to articulate my intellectual interests, analytical competencies, and the empirical work I undertake within applied economics and public policy.

As a penultimate-year student at the University of Sydney, my academic inquiry focuses on the intersection of market structure, regulatory design, and macroeconomic stability. I am fascinated by the mechanisms through which statutory frameworks influence firm behavior, market concentration, and long-run resource allocation. My goal is to apply quantitative econometrics alongside institutional economic theory to address questions of market efficiency and deadweight welfare loss.

Whether you are an industry practitioner, economic consultant, researcher, or fellow student, I invite you to explore my core focus areas and reach out. I welcome discussions regarding graduate pathways, research collaborations, or commercial economic strategy.

Sincerely,

Thomas Lin
Applied Economics & Macroeconomic Policy
The University of Sydney
