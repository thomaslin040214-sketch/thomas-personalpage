Welcome & Academic Overview

Candidate for BA in Economics • The University of Sydney

Sydney, Australia

Dear Visitor,

Thank you for visiting my personal academic and research page. I designed this platform to present my intellectual trajectory, analytical background, and empirical inquiries within applied economics and macroeconomic policy design.

As a penultimate-year student at the University of Sydney, my academic inquiry focuses on the intersection of market structure, regulatory design, and macroeconomic stability. I am fascinated by the mechanisms through which statutory frameworks influence firm behavior, market concentration, and long-run resource allocation. My objective is to apply quantitative econometrics alongside institutional economic theory to address questions of market efficiency and deadweight welfare loss.

Whether you are an industry practitioner, economic consultant, researcher, or fellow student, I invite you to explore my core focus areas and reach out. I welcome discussions regarding graduate pathways, research collaborations, or commercial economic strategy.

Sincerely,
